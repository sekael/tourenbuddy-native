Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

TourenBuddy helps you plan mountain tours with your friends, on iPhone, Android and the web. It is a free, non-commercial project, offered in Switzerland. This page explains which personal data we process, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Who Is Responsible

Selim Kälin, 3904 Naters, Switzerland (“we”). For anything about your data, write to feedback@tourenbuddy.ch.

What We Process and Why

DataWhy
Your email address, name and, if you add it, your verified phone numberTo sign you in, to show you to your friends, and to let friends find you by your phone number. Anyone signed in to TourenBuddy can check whether a phone number has an account. Your number is shown only to people with a pending friend request from or to you, or who are your friends.
Contacts you add or import (names, phone numbers, email addresses)Your address book in the app, tour partners, and connecting with friends who use TourenBuddy. Only you see your contacts; we never contact the people in them. Numbers are only checked against TourenBuddy accounts. If someone has saved you as a contact and you want that entry about you removed, write to feedback@tourenbuddy.ch.
Tours: names, places, dates, routes and tracks (GPX), activities, notes; your free daysThe app’s purpose. You choose whom you share them with: your friends and tour partners, or keep a tour private.
Photos and PDFs you attach to toursStored with the tour and shown to those who can see it. Photos keep their EXIF data, which can include where and when they were taken, and everyone who can see the tour can read it.
Addresses of calendars you connectTo mark your busy days. We read the calendar and keep only which days are busy, never its events. We store the address itself. It works like a password: anyone who has it can read that calendar.
Inbox entries, notification settings, your devices’ push tokens, app version and platformTo notify you in the app, by push and by email, as you choose
Reports and blocksTo keep TourenBuddy safe
Your acceptance of the Terms of Use: version, time and your account’s email addressTo show that you accepted them. We keep it as long as your account exists, and for 10 years after you delete it, to defend against legal claims.
On the web: your sign-in and language choiceKept in your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to show the app in your language. This is strictly necessary for the service, so we do not ask for consent. We set no tracking cookies.

The map comes straight from swisstopo: your device asks swisstopo for map tiles and place names, which shows swisstopo your IP address and the area you look at. This is not linked to your account. The app does not use your location.

We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Who Processes Data for Us and Who Receives It

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage
  • Cloudflare: this website and the service that sends notifications and syncs calendars
  • Brevo: sending emails
  • Twilio: sending the SMS codes that verify a phone number
  • Apple and Google: delivering push notifications to iPhone and Android
  • swisstopo (Federal Office of Topography): map tiles, place names, elevation and route profiles, requested by your device. Elevation and profile requests include the coordinates of the route you plan.

Supabase stores the app’s data in Switzerland (Zurich). Supabase, Cloudflare, Twilio, Apple and Google are US companies, and data can be accessed from the United States. Cloudflare can also process data on servers in other countries. Data goes to the EU only for Brevo, based in France. For the United States, each transfer relies on the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on standard contractual clauses with the Swiss addition. Under Swiss law, EU countries have adequate data protection.

How Long We Keep Data

As long as your account exists. Inbox entries are deleted after 90 days. When you delete your account, we delete your data, except: reports, kept without a link to you; entries other people made about you themselves, such as you as a contact in their address book; and the record of your acceptance of the Terms of Use, which we keep for 10 years after deletion to defend against legal claims (see Delete account).

Your Rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy of it in a common electronic format (for example JSON; you can also download each tour as GPX in the app), object to processing and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to feedback@tourenbuddy.ch; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Young People

If you are under 18, you need the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not check ages. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account without that consent, we delete the account.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and its date. If a change affects you, we show it to you in the app before it takes effect and ask you to accept the Terms of Use again.
