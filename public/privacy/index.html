Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

TourenBuddy helps you plan mountain tours with your friends, on iPhone, Android and the web. This page explains which personal data we process, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The operator named in the imprint (“we”). Write to feedback@tourenbuddy.ch for anything about your data.

What we process and why

DataWhy
Your email address, name and, if you add it, your verified phone numberTo sign you in, to show you to your friends, and to let friends find you by your phone number
Contacts you add or import (names, phone numbers, email addresses)Your address book in the app, tour partners, and connecting with friends who use TourenBuddy. Only you see your contacts; we never contact the people in them
Tours: names, places, dates, routes and tracks (GPX), activities, notes; your free daysThe app’s purpose. You choose whom you share them with: your friends and the partners of a tour
Photos and PDFs you attach to toursStored with the tour and shown to those who can see it
Addresses of calendars you connectTo mark your busy days. We read the calendar and keep only which days are busy, never its events
Inbox entries, notification settings, your devices’ push tokens, app version and platformTo notify you in the app, by push and by email, as you choose
Reports and blocksTo keep TourenBuddy safe

The map comes straight from swisstopo: your device asks swisstopo for map tiles and place names, which shows swisstopo your IP address and the area you look at. This is not linked to your account. The app does not use your location.

We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies: providing the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b)), your consent for push notifications and for importing contacts (Art. 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in a safe and secure service for reports, blocks and security logs (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage
  • Cloudflare: this website and the service that sends notifications and syncs calendars
  • Brevo: sending emails
  • Twilio: sending the SMS codes that verify a phone number
  • Apple and Google: delivering push notifications to iPhone and Android
  • swisstopo (Federal Office of Topography): map tiles and place search, requested by your device

Some of them process data outside Switzerland and the EU, in particular in the United States. Where that country has no adequate level of protection, the transfer relies on standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

As long as your account exists. Inbox entries are deleted after 90 days. When you delete your account, everything is deleted, except reports, which are kept without a link to you (see Delete your account).

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive it in a portable format, object to processing and withdraw a consent at any time. Write to feedback@tourenbuddy.ch. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, to your data protection authority.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and its date. Important changes are announced in the app.
