Delete your account

Last updated: 9 November 2026

You can delete your TourenBuddy account at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

Open Profile, tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm. This works on iPhone, Android and the web, and needs an internet connection. You are signed out right away.

Without the app

Write to feedback@tourenbuddy.ch from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm your request by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and how you sign in (password, passkeys)
  • Your profile and your phone number
  • Your tours with their tracks, notes and attachments
  • Your contacts, friendships and friend requests
  • Your free days and connected calendars
  • Your inbox, notification settings and your devices’ push tokens

What is kept

  • Reports you made or that concern you, without your name or account, so that abuse can still be handled
  • What other people saved themselves, such as you as a contact in their address book
